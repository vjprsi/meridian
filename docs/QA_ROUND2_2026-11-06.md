## 4. Genuine findings (design-level — no crash, no money bug, no data leak)

- **F-REF (MEDIUM): create-time referential integrity not enforced.** A customer accepts a **nonexistent `legal_entity_id`** (→ 201); a deposit account accepts a **nonexistent `customer_id`** (→ 201). Recommend `422`/`404` on an unknown parent at create. (Cross-service existence check — touches customer-service and the account service; a deliberate design decision today, so flagged rather than changed under the live demo.)
- **F-CTY (LOW): country codes not ISO-validated.** `nationality="ZZ"` accepted; `residence_country` optional at create. Recommend ISO-3166-1 validation + required-at-create per KYC policy.
- **F4 (LOW, pre-existing): sub-collection GET returns `200 {data:[]}` for a nonexistent parent** instead of 404 (21 endpoints). Within-tenant empty result, no cross-tenant leak — REST-convention choice.
- **F2 (MEDIUM, pre-existing): `/v1/payment-orders/{id}/events`** returns a bare `[]` with no tenant guard / non-standard envelope (hand-written ETM controller). Empty today, latent isolation gap — route through the standard CRUD plumbing.

## 5. Verdict
The pagination/URI defect is fixed, deployed fleet-wide, and pushed. Money movement, overdraft protection, concurrency safety, tenant isolation, idempotency, email/phone/amount/currency validation, transaction-history reflection, ledger balance, and the error contract all behave correctly for a banking core. The remaining items are create-time referential/format validation hardening, not correctness or safety bugs.
